1. Core Definitions & Laws

High Yield

Coulomb's Law

Source: Ch 12, p. 14

The electrostatic force between two point charges is directly proportional to the product of the magnitudes of charges and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between their centers.

Vector Formulation
F⃗ = (1 / 4πε₀) · (q₁q₂ / r²) r̂
Where ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² C²/N·m² (Permittivity of Free Space)

Electric Field Intensity (E)

Source: Ch 12, p. 14

The force experienced per unit positive test charge placed at that point in an electrostatic field.

Magnitude Formula E⃗ = F⃗ / q₀ N/C or V/m
warning
Frequent Board Exam Trap!

Remember that dielectric constant εᵣ > 1 always reduces the electrostatic force (F′ = F / εᵣ) and field, while strictly increasing capacitance (C′ = εᵣ C).
