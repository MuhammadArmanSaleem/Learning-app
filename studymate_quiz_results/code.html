Q1: If the distance between two point charges is doubled, what happens to the electrostatic force?

check_circle Correct
Your Answer: It is reduced to one-fourth (F/4)
Explanation

By Coulomb's inverse-square law, F is inversely proportional to r². When distance becomes 2r, (2r)² = 4r², reducing force to F/4.
