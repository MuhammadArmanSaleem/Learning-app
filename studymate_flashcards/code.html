Electric Potential & Capacitance

Define Electric Potential Difference between two points in an electric field and state its SI unit with formula.

Governing Equation ΔV = W / q₀
SI Unit Volt (J • C⁻¹)
lightbulb Board Exam Hint expand_more

Work done per unit positive test charge against electrostatic repulsion to maintain electrostatic equilibrium.

check_circle Marking Scheme Answer 4 Marks (FBISE Section B)

Definition:

The work done by an external agent in moving a unit positive test charge q₀ from point A to B against the electric field while keeping the charge in electrostatic equilibrium.

Mathematical Form:
V_B - V_A = ΔV = W_AB / q₀

1 Volt = 1 Joule of work done on 1 Coulomb charge (1 V = 1 J C⁻¹).
