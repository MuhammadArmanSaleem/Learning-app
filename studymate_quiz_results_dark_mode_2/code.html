Question 1 check_circle Correct

If the distance between two charges is doubled, what happens to the electrostatic force between them?

Your Answer: It is reduced to one-fourth (F/4)

According to Coulomb's Law, force is inversely proportional to the square of the distance (F ∝ 1/r²). Doubling r makes F become F/4.

Question 2 cancel Incorrect

What is the SI unit of Electric Flux (Φ)?

Your Answer: N/C
Correct Answer: N·m²/C

Electric flux is given by Φ = E·A. Since E is measured in N/C and area A in m², the unit evaluates to N·m²/C (or V·m).

Question 3 check_circle Correct

What is the electric field intensity inside a hollow charged spherical conductor?

Your Answer: Zero

By Gauss's Law, no charge is enclosed within the interior Gaussian surface, resulting in zero internal electric field (E = 0).
